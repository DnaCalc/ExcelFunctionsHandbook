# Handbook Gneiss Profile — Experimental Design

Status: `designed`, not yet an earned conformance claim

This profile declares how The Handbook of Excel Functions intends to realize a Gneiss Knowledge
System. It is committed now so the ledger bootstrap and site generator have a boundary to satisfy.
H3 will turn the profile's load-bearing statements into Gneiss answers; H4 will publish the machine
manifest and drill results.

## Category Claim

The Handbook is **designed as a Gneiss Knowledge System**. After the ledger-backed vertical slice
and presentation drills run, the strongest permitted interim badge is **Experimental Gneiss
Knowledge System**. No `Gneiss-conformant` badge is claimed until a ratified profile and its
versioned conformance kit exist.

## Covered Scope

Gneiss governs published, load-bearing Handbook answers about:

- function/operator identity, admission, classification, and curated signature claims;
- implementation existence, flavour, verification, and compatibility warrant;
- suite publication and oracle provenance;
- curated-page and Last Bit episode existence;
- current contest, staleness, supersession, and explicit insufficiency.

Git, OxFunc projections, source repositories, vector suites, documents, and plates remain evidence
organs. Ordinary teaching prose is curated content unless it makes an independently challengeable,
load-bearing claim.

## Mode and Boundary

- **Mode:** foundation for the claims plane; external evidence organs for bulk material.
- **Default public view:** `public-current` (to be declared and pinned in H3).
- **Writer:** `efh` only.
- **Domain owner:** the Handbook owns `efh.*`, Excel observation contexts, suite warrant types, and
  public terminology.
- **Substrate owner:** Gneiss owns assertion, decision, context, belief, receipt, staleness, contest,
  typed-missing, and generic answer-envelope semantics.

## Presentation Lenses

The same evaluated answer is available through two presentation lenses:

| Lens | Contract |
|---|---|
| `working` | Compact domain terminology; every label is true without expansion |
| `explicit` | Expands registered qualifications inline with scope and exclusions in plain language |

The lens is presentation state, not an evaluation context. Both lenses must carry the same answer
identity, context hash, result hash, and receipt. The versioned registry is
`content/vocabulary/working-terms.json`.

## Required Doors

Every load-bearing answer exposes, within two gestures: Why, Sources, As of, Rules, History,
Limits, and Replay. A challenge affordance submits testimony; it does not mutate current belief.

## Planned Version Pins

The generated profile will pin:

- Gneiss protocol/profile version;
- Gneiss.Cell package or commit and evaluator version;
- Handbook vocabulary and explanation-registry versions;
- ledger high-water/context declaration;
- site renderer version and publication input hash.

## Initial Drills

1. **Same answer, two lenses:** Working and Explicit produce the same answer/result/receipt ids.
2. **Compact-label honesty:** every rendered registered term has its required scope fields and can
   stand alone without its expansion.
3. **Explicit completeness:** every registered term renders plain meaning, boundary, and declared
   exclusions without hand-authored page duplication.
4. **Seven doors:** each load-bearing answer reaches all required surfaces; no decorative dead end.
5. **Stale/contest parity:** both lenses change status together when the same answer becomes stale,
   contested, defeated, or missing.
6. **Replay:** a published answer re-evaluates under its pinned Gneiss and Handbook versions or
   reports exactly which input no longer survives.

## Known Pre-Implementation Limits

- The H3 ledger and `efh` generator do not yet exist.
- The mockups exercise presentation only; their receipts are illustrative.
- No Gneiss conformance profile has been ratified.
- Working-term registry validation is not yet wired into CI.

## Realization Learning

Handbook discoveries that appear generic are exercised locally first and handed to Gneiss through
`docs/handoffs/gneiss/`. Gneiss decides their generic status. Excel-specific terms never enter the
Gneiss kernel merely because this realization needs them.
