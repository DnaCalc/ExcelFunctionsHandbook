# PRICE

## What it does

`PRICE(settlement,maturity,rate,yld,redemption,frequency,[basis])` returns a coupon security’s clean price per 100 face value from an annual yield. Rate is the annual coupon rate; frequency is one, two or four payments per year.

## Try it

`=PRICE(DATE(2027,1,1),DATE(2028,1,1),0.06,0.06,100,2,0)` is about `100`. Settlement is on a coupon date, coupon and yield both correspond to six percent, and redemption is par.

## Quirks

Basis defaults to zero: 0 US 30/360, 1 actual/actual, 2 actual/360, 3 actual/365, 4 European 30/360. Dates and selectors are truncated under their documented rules. Use DATE values rather than locale-dependent date text. Settlement must precede maturity; negative coupon rate, invalid frequency/basis or nonpositive redemption gives `#NUM!`. The documented PRICE domain rejects negative yield. Clean price excludes accrued coupon interest.

## How to implement

Build coupon dates backward from maturity using frequency and month-end rules. Let A be accrued days, DSC days to the next coupon, E the relevant coupon length and N the remaining coupon count. Discount each coupon `100*rate/frequency` and redemption by `1+yld/frequency` at exponents beginning with `DSC/E`, then subtract `coupon*A/E`. The one-coupon branch uses its own simple-discount formula rather than the general power sum. In the example there is no accrued interest and two coupons of three. Day-count, power and reduction last bits remain partly open in the recorded reports.
