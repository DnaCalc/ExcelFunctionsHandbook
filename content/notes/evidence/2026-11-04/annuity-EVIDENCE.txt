What blocks a landable patch
----------------------------
PMT's em = (1+r)^-n - 1 (F2). Every derived function goes through it, so IPMT/PPMT/CUM* answers
cannot be matched in the bank until it is known. The corpus cells (literal, ordinary rates) are
also blocked: PPMT's own discount factor differs from PMT's em (F4), and no tested family
reproduces it.

Minimal Excel questions (none taken; Excel not driven)
------------------------------------------------------
Q1  (where does em's extra error enter?) For the 27 nper=1 bank rows with exact 1+rate that miss
    today (list from emdump.txt, column 3 = Excel em): =EXP(-LN(1+rate)) and =LN(EXP(-LN(1+rate))).
    If both equal excel_exp/excel_log bits, the error is in how expm1 combines them, not in its
    inputs.
Q2  (is PMT's t = -nper*L?) Same rates, nper = 2 and 4, pv = 1 + j*2^-52 for j = 0..7.
    Doubling is exact, so if t = -n*L the pinned em must match Kahan(2t), Kahan(4t); a mismatch
    pattern unlike nper=1 shows a different exponent route.
Q3  (PPMT's discount factor) =PPMT(r,1,1,pv) and =PPMT(r,1,2,pv) for 16 rates, pv=1. The exact
    answers are -pv and -pv/(2+r); P - I and P*v^m forms give different bits, which tells
    which identity Excel evaluates.
Q4  (does PPMT reuse PMT?) =PPMT(r,k,n,pv) beside =PMT(r,n,pv)*POWER(1+r,k-1-n) and
    =PMT(r,n,pv)+pv*r (k=1) for the 30-year loan.
