Findings (exact, reproducible from files in this folder)

F1. PMT consumer order, fv=0, type 0, rate>0 (structtest.py, einv.py; data base-PMT.jsonl).
    4,802 bank groups share (rate, nper) with 8+ different pv each. Holding one free binary64 em
    per group, the four consumer orders were tested exactly (Fraction arithmetic):
      q=pv/em then q*rate .................. consistent in every sampled group
      rate/em then pv*k, pv*rate then /em, em/rate then pv/k .... 8-12 of 40 groups
    Precision of the two steps (all 4,802 groups):
      x87-stored quotient, x87-stored product (XX) ... 0 groups without a consistent em
      binary64 quotient, x87 product (current) ....... 59 groups without one
      binary64 both ................................. 111 groups without one
    So Excel stores RN53(RN64(pv/em)) then RN53(RN64(q*rate)), with em a stored binary64.
    em is pinned uniquely in 4,720 groups (edata.txt: rate, nper, em bits).

F2. em itself is not identified. Against the 4,720 pinned values (probe_em.rs):
      current chain, Kahan expm1 of t = -(nper*log1p(rate)) ............ 3,258
      same with x87 n*L, any exp backend (x87, CRT, portable) x any log ... 3,248-3,258
      Kahan log1p (u=1+r, log(u)*r/(u-1)) in 6 orders ..................... <= 2,740
      t kept extended (3 log routes, u stored or extended, 4 orders) ...... <= 3,142
      fully extended x87 chains (FYL2X/FYL2XP1, F2XM1 direct or 2^y-1) .... <= 2,593
      complement of growth (-(g-1)/g, 1/g-1, three precisions) ........... below 3,258
      log via stored log2 times ln2 (3 precisions), log(1+r) ............... <= 3,057
    Every one of the 1,462 misses is reproduced by the current Kahan expm1 at t shifted by 1 or 2
    units in the last place (-2:46, -1:442, +1:845, +2:94; none needs more). Excel's em errs up to
    3 ulp from the exact value and leans to larger magnitude (-1: 1,176 rows, +1: 762), so it is
    less accurate than the current chain, not more. Misses persist when 1+rate is exact in binary64
    (27 of 95 such nper=1 rows), so a log1p routine is not the whole cause.

F3. Measured consequence: applying F1 alone (x87 quotient in the discount arm) gives
    PMT 222,823 -> 222,961 (+168 / -30), all changes in the fv=0, type 0, rate>0 shape. The 30
    losses are rows where today's binary64 quotient cancels an em error. Not landable under the
    zero-loss rule until em is identified.
    command: same judge with the one-line change, then
             xtask-harness witness compare --before ../traces/base-PMT.jsonl --after ../traces/xq-PMT.jsonl

F4. Derived functions, 30-year loan block of the catalogue (sheet dcj9k_annuity rows 726-752):
    - CUMPRINC(1..12) equals the left-to-right binary64 sum of Excel's own twelve PPMT answers, and
      CUMIPMT equals 12*PMT - CUMPRINC (both bit for bit); the product already uses this shape.
    - PPMT is not PMT - IPMT: P - IPMT = PPMT in 4 of 12 periods, P - PPMT = IPMT in 8 of 12.
    - PPMT(.,1,.) = 0x...723 is not P_double - I_double for any binary64 P (the result would sit on
      the 2^-43 grid), not RN53(RN64(q*r) - RN64(pv*r)), and not any of 13 products P*(1+em) for
      either em consistent with PMT (those give ...720/721 and ...724-726): PPMT does not reuse
      PMT's em. Its relative error, about +4.6e-16 on all twelve periods, exceeds PMT's 1.7e-16.
    - probe_pp.rs: 9 discount routes x 5-6 consumer orders for PPMT and 3 for IPMT, by argument
      shape (pp-in.jsonl, 416 catalogue and bank rows). Nothing separates from chance: PPMT type 0,
      fv 0, middle periods match 10 of 41 for every route and order, and so does the product.
      IPMT is best as it stands except in shapes of 3-17 rows.

What blocks a landable patch
----------------------------
PMT's em = (1+r)^-n - 1 (F2). Every derived function goes through it, so IPMT/PPMT/CUM* answers
cannot be matched in the bank until it is known. The corpus cells (literal, ordinary rates) are
also blocked: PPMT's own discount factor differs from PMT's em (F4), and no tested family
reproduces it.

Minimal Excel questions (none taken; Excel not driven)
------------------------------------------------------
Q1  (where does em's extra error enter?) For the 27 nper=1 bank rows with exact 1+rate that miss
    today (list from emdump.txt, column 3 = Excel em): =EXP(-LN(1+rate)) and =LN(EXP(-LN(1+rate))).
    If both equal excel_exp/excel_log bits, the error is in how expm1 combines them, not in its
    inputs.
Q2  (is PMT's t = -nper*L?) Same rates, nper = 2 and 4, pv = 1 + j*2^-52 for j = 0..7.
    Doubling is exact, so if t = -n*L the pinned em must match Kahan(2t), Kahan(4t); a mismatch
    pattern unlike nper=1 shows a different exponent route.
Q3  (PPMT's discount factor) =PPMT(r,1,1,pv) and =PPMT(r,1,2,pv) for 16 rates, pv=1. The exact
    answers are -pv and -pv/(2+r); P - I and P*v^m forms give different bits, which tells
    which identity Excel evaluates.
Q4  (does PPMT reuse PMT?) =PPMT(r,k,n,pv) beside =PMT(r,n,pv)*POWER(1+r,k-1-n) and
    =PMT(r,n,pv)+pv*r (k=1) for the 30-year loan.

This section supersedes "Outcome: blocked" above. Corrections to the text above:
- F2 "a log1p routine is not the whole cause" was wrong. One double L per rate explains every
  nper (1,622/1,622 and 1,849/1,849), and that L is a non-correctly-rounded series.
- F4 "PPMT does not reuse PMT's em" was only half right: PPMT uses exp(t), not 1+em.

Commits: 08ea3d7a (PMT logarithm and quotient), 6c6cbd31 (IPMT/PPMT), both dc-j9k.12.

Identified (type 0, -1/3 <= rate <= 1/2), RN = RN53(RN64(.)):
  L     = 2s + 2s*(s^2*T), s = r/(2+r), T = 1/3 + s^2/5 + ... + s^20/23, all binary64
  em_m  = x87 Kahan expm1(-(m*L)); u_m = x87 exp(-(m*L))
  q     = RN(N/em_n)
  PMT   = RN(q*r)
  IPMT  = -RN(RN(RN(q*em_m) - RN(fv*u_m))*r)
  PPMT  = RN(RN(RN(q-fv)*u_m)*r)
  fv 0, type 0 scores: PMT 11,706/11,712 (5% sample of the bank) and 720/720 fresh Excel;
  IPMT 95/95; PPMT 74/74 and 64/64 fresh Excel.
